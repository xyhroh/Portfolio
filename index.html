My Design Process

  1. 01

    Test before I polish

    Sketch it, put it in front of users, change what fails. On EXHI we proved the checkout flow on paper before any visual design.

  2. 02

    Design for real constraints

    Phone cameras in bowling alleys ruled out motion capture on Pinpoint, so I scoped the feature to what the hardware could actually do.

  3. 03

    Build with the team

    3 years coordinating cross-functional teams in RSAF security ops. I hand engineers flows they can build against, not just mockups.
