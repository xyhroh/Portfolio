About

From buildings to screens, I design for the people using them.

I trained in Environment Design at Temasek Polytechnic, shaping spaces around how people actually move through them. The medium changed when I moved into UX; the goal didn't. Now at DigiPen Institute of Technology (User Experience & Game Design, 2024–2028), I bring that same focus to digital products, aiming to build human-centred services for public good.

How I work

  1. Sketch
  2. Test with users
  3. Change what fails
  4. Ship

On HealthBuddy I was the sole designer and researcher, from survey to hi-fi prototype. On Pinpoint the first prototype tested as too game-like for a utility app, so I rebuilt it.

Before design school

3 years as a Security Administrator in the Republic of Singapore Air Force: analysing incident reports, gathering requirements from operations and command staff, and leading a team through time-critical system upgrades. It taught me how constrained systems and shift-based teams really work.
