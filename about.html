About

I'm Javier, a UX designer who starts with what users actually do, not what we assume they do.

I'm studying User Experience and Game Design at DigiPen Institute of Technology, Singapore (2024–2028), and I want to build human-centred digital services for public good.

I work in fast research-to-prototype loops: sketch it, put it in front of users, change what fails, ship the next version. On HealthBuddy I was the sole designer and researcher, from a 17-person survey and 3 personas to a hi-fi prototype. On Pinpoint, the first prototype tested as too game-like for a utility app, so I rebuilt it; 5 of 5 bowlers then chose the new build.

Before design school I spent three years as a Security Administrator in the Republic of Singapore Air Force, analysing incident reports, gathering requirements from operations and command staff, and leading a team through time-critical system upgrades. It taught me how constrained systems and shift-based teams really work.

I also built this site myself, in Three.js and GSAP, and usability tested it with 10 designers and students.
